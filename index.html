About Medusa

A beachside sanctuary where Mediterranean flavors meet the Red Sea breeze.

Medusa brings together the best of Mediterranean cuisine with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients and a relaxed atmosphere. Our terrace offers stunning sea views while you enjoy our signature dishes, from grilled seafood to authentic mezze platters.

Whether you're here for a leisurely breakfast, a sunset dinner, or evening drinks, Medusa provides the perfect setting for unforgettable moments by the sea.
